For the week ending September 25, 2026, U.S. refineries processed 16.3 million barrels per day (b/d), down 554,000 b/d from the previous week, at 92.5% capacity utilization. Gasoline output averaged 9.5 million b/d, and distillate production decreased to 5.0 million b/d. 

Crude oil imports decreased 179,000 b/d to 5.7 million b/d, although the four-week average of 6.4 million b/d is 4.8% above the year-ago level. Gasoline imports averaged 500,000 b/d; distillate imports averaged 153,000 b/d. 

Commercial crude oil inventories (excluding the Strategic Petroleum Reserve) increased 0.9 million barrels to 427.3 million barrels, 2% above the five-year average. Gasoline inventories decreased 1.7 million barrels, 7% below the five-year average. Distillate inventories decreased 2.3 million barrels, 14% below the five-year average. Propane/propylene inventories increased 1.8 million barrels, 20% above the five-year average. Total commercial petroleum inventories decreased by 7.0 million barrels for the week.

Over the past four weeks, total product supplied averaged 20.8 million b/d, up 2.1% year over year. The four-week average for gasoline product supplied increased 0.3% year over year to 8.7 million b/d, while the four-week average for distillate product supplied increased 5.2% to 3.8 million b/d. The four-week average for jet fuel product supplied increased 6.5% year over year.
